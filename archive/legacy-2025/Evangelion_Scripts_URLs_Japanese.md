## Alternative Sources for Japanese Subtitles

While full Japanese scripts are limited, Japanese subtitles (字幕) may be available from:

1. **Kitsunekko.net** - Japanese subtitle database
   - URL: https://kitsunekko.net/
   - Contains .srt and .ass subtitle files for anime
   - May require manual browsing

2. **OpenSubtitles.org**
   - Has subtitle collections for Evangelion films
   - Eva 3.0: https://www.opensubtitles.org/en/search/sublanguageid-all/idmovie-47864

3. **Official Blu-ray/DVD Releases**
   - Japanese home video releases include Japanese closed captions
   - These can be extracted using subtitle extraction tools

4. **Fan Subtitle Groups**
   - Chinese subtitle group Kamigami (诸神字幕组) has releases with Japanese subtitles
   - URL: https://subs.kamigami.org/276.html
   - Includes 序 (1.11), 破 (2.22), and Q (3.33) with external Japanese subtitles

## Notes

- Japanese scripts (台本) are much less commonly shared than subtitles (字幕)
- Official scripts are proprietary to Studio Khara
- Subtitle files (.srt, .ass) contain dialog but may not include all scene descriptions
- For movies 1.11, 2.22, and 3.0+1.0, you may need to:
  - Extract subtitles from official Blu-ray releases
  - Check Japanese subtitle databases like kitsunekko.net
  - Use streaming platforms that offer Japanese closed captions
